# Inside Your Devices: Lesson Blueprint v1.1 audit (main, worktree `cnt-next` @ 2542014)

## Summary

None of the 7 Inside Your Devices lessons on `main` meets Lesson Blueprint v1.1 (none has `"blueprint": "1.1"`). The hands-on parts are strong: real teardowns, explore-mode scenes, simulators, sort bins and scenarios, all with hints and nudges, and the safety framing is good. The lesson structure is the problem. **No lesson has a Hook or an unscored Predict card.** Three lessons run 12–16 cards, against a limit of 8. The only lesson guests can play, `whats-in-the-box`, is 16 cards (about 11 min at 45 s per core card) and opens with "Tap the glowing part." with no situation. Lessons 2 and 3 open with "Tap the word." Most question cards are text only: true/false, fill the gap, multiple choice and scenarios. 4 true/false cards and 10 scenario steps offer only 2 answers. More than 20 explanations go over 26 words (the longest is 61). Several prompts give the answer away by stating the rule (`wont-charge`, `helps-or-harms`, `delete-or-keep`, `fake-virus-popup`). The quizzes are in decent shape (7–8 cards, scene, simulator and order cards). **Access:** module 1 "Pull It Apart" is free; modules 2 "Software in Charge" and 3 "Fix It Yourself" are Pro, with teasers `end-the-frozen-app` and `free-up-space`. Guests can play only `whats-in-the-box`.

Legend: ✅ pass · 🟡 partial · ❌ fail. "Cards" = total (core). Prompt limit is 12 words, the explanation limit is 2 sentences or 26 words. Scroll risk is a judgement call, not measured: `npm run e2e:fit-audit COURSE=inside-your-devices` has not been run on this branch.

## Facts and safety that look wrong

- **`meet-the-os/speaker-turns`:** operating systems *mix* several sounds at once. "Three apps driving one speaker at once would clash" is wrong, and the "correct" consequence itself says all three play. Reframe it as "the OS mixes them for you."
- **`power-problems/too-hot-to-handle`:** the explanation says "never in the freezer", but the old freezer choice (id `freezer`) now reads "Keep playing, but turn the brightness down". The explanation and the choices no longer match.
- **`inside-your-devices-final/q-phone-safe-order`:** a five-step real phone-opening sequence (soften glue, lift the back, bracket, battery, camera) reads like a how-to. That breaks the physical-safety rule "never instructions for opening a real device".
- **`meet-the-cpu/add-cores`:** "12 s, 6 s, 3 s" is perfectly linear. Real video export doesn't scale like that, so say "about".
- **`power-problems/last-the-journey`:** the explanation ranks location alongside brightness as a top drain. That's overstated (the screen and mobile signal usually dominate). This is minor.

## 10 biggest weaknesses (ranked by risk of losing a brand-new 13+ learner arriving from a TikTok video)

1. **The guest lesson is 16 cards with no hook.** `whats-in-the-box` is the only lesson guests can play: about 11 min, and it opens on `meet-the-battery` "Tap the glowing part." with no situation. **Fix:** split it into two or three blueprint lessons of 6–8 cards (open the laptop / same jobs in a phone), each starting with a Hook ("Your laptop dies mid-game. What's inside?").
2. **There are 0 Hook and 0 unscored Predict cards in the course.** Nothing asks "what do you think happens?" before teaching. **Fix:** add a Hook and a "Not sure" Predict to every lesson, for example `meet-the-cpu`: "1 core or 4: which saves the video first?" before `add-cores`.
3. **Lessons open on "Tap the word."** `memory-vs-storage/meet-ram-the-desk` and `meet-the-cpu/meet-ghz` start with a vocabulary card that has no picture or story. **Fix:** open on a play-mode `fill-the-ram` simulator or a laggy-game hook instead, and name the term after it.
4. **It feels like a worksheet.** About 25 graded lesson cards have no picture: every true/false, fill-the-gap, MC and scenario (for example `apps-need-ram`, `why-restart-helps`, `split-or-not`, `os-or-app`, `helps-or-harms`). **Fix:** make them picture-first (a device mockup or scene with answers on the picture, using MC `area`), or cut them.
5. **Read-to-answer prompts give the answer away.** `wont-charge` ("Tip: … metal or water"), `helps-or-harms`, `delete-or-keep`, `fake-virus-popup`, and `phone-ram-swap` (the previous card says the answer). **Fix:** keep the prompt as the situation only, and move the rule into a Name-it card after a Predict.
6. **Explanations are walls of text.** `too-hot` 61 words, `fill-the-ram` 53, `find-the-cooling` 51, `open-the-phone` 46, `label-the-laptop` 42, plus about 15 more over 26. **Fix:** one WHY line, plus a `showAnswer` line for the 2nd wrong try.
7. **Many questions have only 2 answers.** 4 true/false cards (`phone-ram-swap`, `apps-need-ram`, `more-ghz-more-steps`, `q-close-apps-download`) and 10 two-choice scenario steps are 50/50 guesses, against the 3+ rule. **Fix:** add a real misconception as a third option, or turn the true/false cards into 3-option MC.
8. **Too many ideas per lesson.** `meet-the-cpu` (GHz + cores + cooling), `meet-the-os` (5 topics) and `power-problems` (4 topics) feel long and blur together. **Fix:** split each into one-idea lessons of 6–8 cards (the course has room: M2 and M3 have only 2 lessons each).
9. **Tall cards risk scrolling at 360×560, including a Pro teaser.** `end-the-frozen-app` (the M2 Pro teaser: 20-word prompt + device + bar + 4-row list), `helps-or-harms` (25w + 6 items), `memory-leak`, `free-up-space`, `last-the-journey`, `label-the-laptop`. **Fix:** cut prompts to ≤12 words, trim rows and controls, and run `e2e:fit-audit COURSE=inside-your-devices`.
10. **Unreadable clue text in the file-browser scene.** `files-and-folders/label-the-files` shows `photo.jpg.exe` at ≈10–11px on a phone, and reading that name is the whole task. **Fix:** draw the scene text at 15+ units with 3 rows. Also give `meet-the-os`, `files-and-folders`, `slow-and-full` and `power-problems` the missing "Try this" and/or "Next:" Wrap lines.
