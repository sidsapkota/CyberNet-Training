## 1 · `q-share-or-private` (digital footprint)
- **Situation:** You're filling in a new public profile.
- **Picture:** Sort bins "Fine to share" and "Keep private", each item with a small icon (guitar,
  school building, map pin, paw print).
- **Action:** Drag each into Fine to share or Keep private.
- **Answers:** "Favourite hobby" · "Your school's name" · "Where you are right now" · "First pet's name"
- **Correct:** Fine to share: favourite hobby. Keep private: your school's name, where you are right
  now, first pet's name.
- **Explanation:** A school and where you are can find you, and a first pet's name can unlock a password
  reset.

## 2 · `q-old-photo` (digital footprint)
- **Situation:** Jo deleted an embarrassing photo last year. Now it's back online.
- **Picture:** Jo's phone with "Post deleted · last year", and beside it a different phone showing the
  same photo (a kid mid-sneeze, cartoon style) shared in a group chat today.
- **Action:** Pick how it came back.
- **Answers:** "Someone saved a copy before it was deleted" · "Deleted posts come back after a year" ·
  "The app puts popular posts back up"
- **Correct:** Someone saved a copy before it was deleted
- **Explanation:** Deleting removes your copy, but saved copies and screenshots can last.

## 3 · `q-settings` (privacy settings)
- **Situation:** Four problems on a public account, and four privacy settings.
- **Picture:** A Settings screen with four switches, each shown with its name only.
- **Action:** Match each problem to the switch that fixes it.
- **Answers:** "Strangers see all your posts" · "Strangers keep messaging" · "An app shows where you
  are" · "You're tagged without asking" ↔ "Private account" · "Messages: friends only" · "Share location
  off" · "Review tags"
- **Correct:** in that order
- **Explanation:** Each switch controls one thing: posts, messages, location or tags.

## 4 · `q-stranger` (privacy settings)
- **Situation:** Someone from a game chat offers a gift, then asks your suburb.
- **Picture:** A chat with "GiftBuddy_4" (a gift-box avatar, no face): "i'll send u a gift! what suburb r
  u in? send a pic so i know it's u". The chat's "⋯" menu shows Block and Report.
- **Action:** Pick what you do.
- **Answers:** "Send your suburb, not a photo" · "Make your account private, then reply" · "Block,
  report and tell a trusted adult"
- **Correct:** Block, report and tell a trusted adult
- **Explanation:** A suburb and a photo can find you; block, report and tell a trusted adult, and you're
  not in trouble.

## 5 · `q-permissions` (app permissions)
- **Situation:** Four apps each ask for one thing.
- **Picture:** Sort bins "Needs it" and "Doesn't need it"; each item is an app icon, an arrow and a
  permission icon.
- **Action:** Drag each into Needs it or Doesn't need it.
- **Answers:** "Camera app → camera" · "Running route map → location" · "Alarm clock → contacts" ·
  "Calculator → microphone"
- **Correct:** Needs it: camera app → camera, running route map → location. Doesn't need it: alarm clock →
  contacts, calculator → microphone.
- **Explanation:** Allow what fits the app's job: an alarm clock or calculator has no use for your
  contacts or mic.

## 6 · `q-while-using` (app permissions)
- **Situation:** A bus timetable app asks for your location to find nearby stops.
- **Picture:** The app with a map of bus stops and a pop-up with three buttons: **Always (even when
  closed)**, **Only while using the app**, **Don't allow**.
- **Action:** Tap the best button.
- **Answers:** the three buttons
- **Correct:** Only while using the app
- **Explanation:** It finds your stops while it's open, and can't see where you are once it's closed.

## 7 · `q-pool-wi-fi` (public Wi-Fi)
- **Situation:** At the pool, two Wi-Fi networks show up. You want to watch a video.
- **Picture:** The pool's front desk with a staff member, and your Wi-Fi list: "Pool_Guest" and
  "Pool_Free_WiFi" (more bars).
- **Action:** Pick what you do first.
- **Answers:** "Join the one with more bars" · "Ask staff which is theirs" · "Join the one with Free in
  its name"
- **Correct:** Ask staff which is theirs
- **Explanation:** Anyone can pick a network's name or sit close for strong bars; staff know their own.

## 8 · `q-wi-fi` (public Wi-Fi)
- **Situation:** You need to check your bank account at a café.
- **Picture:** A café table, your phone with the café's Wi-Fi list, and a separate "Mobile data" switch.
- **Action:** Pick the safest way to go online.
- **Answers:** "Any network with the café's name" · "The network with the strongest signal" · "Mobile
  data instead of Wi-Fi"
- **Correct:** Mobile data instead of Wi-Fi
- **Explanation:** On public Wi-Fi you can't always be sure which network is real, so use mobile data
  for money.
