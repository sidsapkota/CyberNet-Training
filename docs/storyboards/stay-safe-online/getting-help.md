# Getting Help (`getting-help`)

- **Module:** 4 · When Things Go Wrong · Free (help module, open to guests)
- **The one idea:** you never have to handle it alone: start with a trusted adult, and there's a right
  helper for every problem.
- **The story:** your phone's "Who can help?" board, a screen of eight helper tiles that stays on
  screen all lesson. Each card puts a different problem at the top of the board.
- **Cards:** 8 · difficulty: Predict easy, Easy win easy, Twist medium, Real life stretch
- **From today's lesson:** split in two. This lesson keeps the id `getting-help` and teaches who to tell
  and where to go. Saving evidence before you block (`block-before-saving`, `never-your-fault`,
  `group-chat`) moves to the new lesson **`save-the-evidence`** (proposed order 4, just before this
  one; this lesson becomes the module's last, order 5). Kept: `talk-to-someone` (folded into Play, so
  Kids Helpline and Lifeline sit side by side), `bank-can-help` (Predict), `who-to-tell` (Easy win),
  `where-to-go` (Twist) and `recap`. New: `who-can-help` (Hook), `help-board` (Play), `meet-helpers`
  (Name it), `fake-shop` (Real life). Folded into the Play board: `meet-report` (Scamwatch),
  `meet-bank-idcare` and `meet-000`. Every service, number and web address is exactly as verified in
  REVIEW.md (ReportCyber at cyber.gov.au stays out, as today, to keep the board short).
- **Needs building:** a new self-labelled scene **`help-board`**: a phone screen titled "Who can
  help?" with eight tiles in two columns (each tile a tappable part with a lucide icon, a name and
  one short line). Explore mode on it exists. Kids Helpline and Lifeline are always side by side, and
  no number breaks across lines.

## 1 · Hook (`who-can-help`)
- **Situation:** Something online is worrying you. It's never your fault.
- **Picture:** a teen sitting on a bed, phone in hand, a thought bubble: "Who do I even tell?" On the
  phone screen, the faint outline of the "Who can help?" board.
- **Action:** Tap to start.

## 2 · Play (`help-board`, not graded: hotspot explore on `help-board`)
- **Situation:** These people are here to help. Tap each one.
- **Picture:** the "Who can help?" board fills the card. Eight tiles, two columns, each with the same
  faint hollow ring:
  - "Trusted adult" (people icon) · "000" (life-buoy icon)
  - "Kids Helpline 1800 55 1800" (phone icon) · "Lifeline 13 11 14" (phone icon), side by side
  - "Your bank" (card icon) · "IDCARE" (shield icon)
  - "Scamwatch" (warning-message icon) · "eSafety" (shield-alert icon)
- **Action:** Tap each tile.
- **What each tap shows:**
  - Trusted adult: "A parent, carer, teacher or school counsellor. Start here for anything."
  - Kids Helpline: "Free, private, any time, for ages 5 to 25."
  - Lifeline: "For anyone in Australia, any time."
  - Your bank: "Money gone from your card? Call straight away."
  - Scamwatch: "Report a scam at scamwatch.gov.au, to warn others."
  - IDCARE: "Your details were stolen? IDCARE (idcare.org) helps you make a plan."
  - eSafety: "Bullying the app won't remove, or a private picture shared? esafety.gov.au."
  - 000: "Someone could get hurt right now? Call 000."
- Continue unlocks once all eight have been tapped.

## 3 · Predict (`bank-can-help`, easy · multiple choice with picture)
Predict: not scored (any answer, including Not sure, goes straight to the reveal; no hint, no 2nd-wrong step; not counted in first-try accuracy).
- **Situation:** A scam took money from your card. You call your bank now.
- **Picture:** the board with the "Your bank" tile lit, and beside it a bank card with a phone
  ringing. → **Reveal:** the card with a lock icon and the word "Blocked", and a payment line reading
  "$30 · Being checked".
- **Action:** Tap what the bank can do.
- **Answers:** "Block the card, and maybe stop the payment" · "Nothing: once money's gone, it's gone"
  · "Tell you off for falling for a scam" · "Not sure"
- **What happens:** "Block the card, and maybe stop the payment".
- **Reveal line:** Here's what happens… calling fast lets the bank block the card and check the payment, and you're not in trouble.

## 4 · Name it (`meet-helpers`, explainer)
- **Says:** You never have to handle it alone. Start with a **trusted adult**, then go to the helper
  made for the problem.
- **Picture:** the same board, now with a small problem tag under each tile: Trusted adult "Anything",
  Kids Helpline and Lifeline "Need to talk", Your bank "Money", Scamwatch "Scams", IDCARE "Stolen
  details", eSafety "Bullying or pictures", 000 "Danger now".

## 5 · Easy win (`who-to-tell`, easy · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** You're worried about something online, but not ready to tell family.
- **Picture:** the board, with the teen from the Hook beside it looking unsure.
- **Action:** Tap who you could talk to.
- **Answers:** "Kids Helpline, or a school counsellor" · "No one, until it gets really bad" ·
  "Strangers on an online forum"
- **Correct:** "Kids Helpline, or a school counsellor"
- **Right:** Kids Helpline and school counsellors are there for you, so you don't have to wait or cope
  alone.
- **1st wrong:** Look at the board: who could you talk to outside your family?
- **2nd wrong:** Kids Helpline or a school counsellor: you don't need a big problem to ask for help.

## 6 · Twist (`where-to-go`, medium · match pairs)
- **Situation:** Four different problems. Each one has its own helper.
- **Picture:** the board shrinks to the top; below it, four problem cards on the left and four helper
  tiles (from the board) on the right.
- **Action:** Match each problem to its helper.
- **Answers (pairs):** "Bullying the app won't remove" → "eSafety" · "Money taken from your card" →
  "Your bank, now" · "Your details were stolen" → "IDCARE" · "Danger right now" → "Call 000"
- **Correct:** as listed.
- **Right:** Each problem has a helper made for it: eSafety, your bank, IDCARE, and 000 for danger.
- **1st wrong:** Start with the one you're surest of. What does each helper's tile say it's for?
- **2nd wrong:** Bullying goes to eSafety, card trouble to your bank, stolen details to IDCARE, and
  danger to 000.

## 7 · Real life (`fake-shop`, stretch · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** A fake shop got your card number and a photo of your ID.
- **Picture:** the board with two problems at the top as small cards: a bank card ("Card number")
  and an ID card ("Photo of your ID"). No tile is lit.
- **Action:** Tap who to contact.
- **Answers:** "Your bank, then IDCARE" · "000, then eSafety" · "Wait to see if any money goes"
- **Correct:** "Your bank, then IDCARE"
- **Right:** Your bank can protect the card, and IDCARE helps with your ID, with a trusted adult
  beside you.
- **1st wrong:** There are two problems here: money and stolen details. Who helps with each?
- **2nd wrong:** Your bank for the card, straight away, then IDCARE for your ID.

## 8 · Wrap (`recap`)
- **Takeaway:** You never have to handle it alone: tell a trusted adult, then the right helper. It's
  never your fault.
- **Try this:** Save **Kids Helpline** (1800 55 1800) and **Lifeline** (13 11 14) in your phone, next
  to the number of someone you trust.
- **Next:** The course final: show everything you've learned.
