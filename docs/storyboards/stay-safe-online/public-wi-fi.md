# Public Wi-Fi (`public-wi-fi`) · NEW lesson, split from `apps-and-wi-fi`

- **Module:** 3 · Guard Your Privacy · Pro
- **The one idea:** On public Wi-Fi anyone can name a network after the café, so ask staff for the real
  one and use mobile data for money and important accounts.
- **The story:** Sam at the Corner Café (fictional) with Sam's phone, its Wi-Fi list always at the top
  of the picture, and the same café behind it: the counter with the café's Wi-Fi box and a staff
  member, table 4 with someone's laptop, the library next door through the window. Each card changes
  one thing: Sam explores the list, joins a network, asks staff, has jobs to do, then you're at an
  airport.
- **Cards:** 7 (Hook and Play share card 1) · difficulty: easy, easy, medium, stretch
- **From today's lesson:** a new lesson (proposed id `public-wi-fi`, order 4 in the module, after
  `apps-and-wi-fi`; `about` "Spot fake Wi-Fi, and keep money off public Wi-Fi.") built from the second
  half of today's `apps-and-wi-fi`. Being a new lesson, it has no saved progress; it reuses the card
  names `wi-fi-wants-password` (now the Predict, with the café picture), `meet-public-wi-fi` (now the
  labelled picture; it takes over `meet-fake-hotspot`, dropped), `wi-fi-ok` (the Twist) and `recap`.
  New: `cafe-networks`, `ask-the-staff`, `airport-game`. One word for the copycat network all course:
  **fake Wi-Fi** (today's "fake hotspot"; "hotspot" also means sharing your phone's data, so it's
  dropped).
- **Needs building:**
  - **A `cafe` scene** (self-labelled: it shows the Wi-Fi list) for an explore-mode hotspot in card 1;
    parts: the café's Wi-Fi box, the laptop at table 4, the library window.
  - **Answers that are tappable parts of a picture** (cards 2, 4 and 6: things in the café, networks in
    the list). Fallback: multiple choice with the picture and short text answers.

## 1 · Hook + Play (shared) · `cafe-networks` · not graded · hotspot explore on `cafe` (new scene)
- **Situation:** Sam's at the Corner Café. Three Wi-Fi networks show up. Tap each.
- **Picture:** Top: Sam's Wi-Fi list with three rows and signal bars: "CornerCafe", "CornerCafe_Free",
  "Library_Guest". Below: the café. Behind the counter, a small box with two aerials; a staff member in
  an apron. At table 4, an open laptop with nobody's face shown. Through the window, the library next
  door. Each tapped thing glows and draws a line to its row in the list, with a one-line callout:
  - Wi-Fi box: "The café's own Wi-Fi. It sends 'CornerCafe'."
  - Laptop: "Someone's laptop. Its owner named its Wi-Fi 'CornerCafe_Free'."
  - Library: "The library next door. It sends 'Library_Guest'."
- **Action:** Tap the glowing things. Continue unlocks once all three are tapped.

## 2 · Predict · `wi-fi-wants-password` · easy · tap a part of the picture (new; fallback multiple choice)
Predict: not scored (any answer, including Not sure, goes straight to the reveal; no hint, no 2nd-wrong step; not counted in first-try accuracy).

- **Reveal line:** Here's what happens… the laptop's owner made this network and its page, and real café Wi-Fi never needs your email password.

## 3 · Name it · `meet-public-wi-fi` · explainer with picture
- **Says:** On **public Wi-Fi**, anyone can name a network after the café. A copycat like
  "CornerCafe_Free" is **fake Wi-Fi**: its name can look just right.
- **Picture:** Card 1's café and list, labelled: the "CornerCafe" row and the Wi-Fi box marked "public
  Wi-Fi"; the "CornerCafe_Free" row and the laptop marked "fake Wi-Fi".

## 4 · Easy win · `ask-the-staff` · easy · tap a part of the picture (new; fallback multiple choice)
- **Situation:** Sam wants the café's real Wi-Fi this time. How can Sam be sure?
- **Picture:** The café counter with the staff member, and Sam's Wi-Fi list above, now with four bars
  on "CornerCafe_Free" and two bars on "CornerCafe". → **Reveal:** the staff member's speech bubble:
  "It's CornerCafe", and that row ticked in the list.
- **Action:** Tap the best way to find its name.
- **Answers:** the staff member · the network with the most bars · the network with "Free" in its name
- **Correct:** the staff member
- **Right:** Staff know their own network's name, so you're not guessing from names and bars.
- **1st wrong:** (most bars) Could a laptop close to Sam have strong bars too? (Free) Who picks a
  network's name?
- **2nd wrong:** Ask the staff: anyone can pick a name, and a laptop nearby can have strong bars.

## 5 · Twist · `wi-fi-ok` · medium · sort bins
- **Situation:** Some jobs are too important for Wi-Fi you can't be sure of.
- **Picture:** Sort bins "Fine on café Wi-Fi" and "Use mobile data", each item with a small icon (play
  button, newspaper, bank card, key).
- **Action:** Drag each job into Fine on café Wi-Fi or Use mobile data.
- **Answers:** "Watching a video" · "Reading the news" · "Checking your bank account" · "Changing an
  important password"
- **Correct:** Fine: watching a video, reading the news. Mobile data: checking your bank account,
  changing an important password.
- **Right:** Everyday browsing is fine; for money and important accounts, mobile data skips any fake
  Wi-Fi.
- **1st wrong:** Which ones involve your money or your most important accounts?
- **2nd wrong:** Videos and news are fine; your bank and important passwords go on mobile data.

## 6 · Real life · `airport-game` · stretch · tap a part of the picture (new; fallback multiple choice)
- **Situation:** At the airport, you want to buy a game on your phone.
- **Picture:** An airport sign "Free Wi-Fi: Airport Wi-Fi". Your phone's list under it: "Airport Wi-Fi"
  (matches the sign), "Airport_Free", and a separate switch "Mobile data". A game shop page waits with a
  **Buy** button. → **Reveal:** "Mobile data" switched on, Wi-Fi off, and the Buy button ticked.
- **Action:** Tap how you'll go online to buy it.
- **Answers:** "Airport Wi-Fi" · "Airport_Free" · "Mobile data"
- **Correct:** Mobile data
- **Right:** Buying is money, so mobile data means you don't have to guess which Wi-Fi is real.
- **1st wrong:** (Airport Wi-Fi) A matching name is great for browsing: what kind of job is buying a
  game? (Airport_Free) Who chose that name, and does it match the sign?
- **2nd wrong:** Mobile data: buying is money, and on public Wi-Fi you can't always be sure which
  network is real.

## 7 · Wrap · `recap`
- **Takeaway:** Anyone can name Wi-Fi after the café: ask staff for the real one, and use mobile data
  for money.
- **Try this:** Next time you're out, ask staff for the Wi-Fi name before you join.
