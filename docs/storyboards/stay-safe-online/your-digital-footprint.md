# Your Digital Footprint (`your-digital-footprint`)

- **Module:** 3 · Guard Your Privacy · Pro
- **The one idea:** Anything you post can be saved by other people and can last, so check it first for
  anything that could find you or unlock your accounts.
- **The story:** Sam (a teen; username **PixelFox**) and Sam's phone with one social app (no brand),
  ringed by four friends' phones. Sam posts one photo after another; each card changes one thing: the
  post spreads, Sam deletes it, a new photo from the front steps, a quiz post, a beach photo.
- **Cards:** 7 (Hook and Play share card 1) · difficulty: easy, easy, medium, stretch
- **From today's lesson:** today's lesson holds two ideas (what to keep private, and privacy
  settings), so it is split: this lesson keeps the id, order 1 and title, with the new `about` "Your
  posts can last, so keep out what finds you or unlocks you."; the settings half becomes the new
  lesson `privacy-settings`. Kept `delete-an-hour-later` (now with a picture and reveal), `meet-footprint`
  (now the labelled picture), `band-name-quiz` (rebuilt as multiple choice with a picture; still the
  module's **teaser card**), `post-while-away` (rebuilt as multiple choice with a picture, now about
  "you"), `recap`. New: `post-and-spread`, `front-steps-photo`. Dropped: `share-or-private` (the
  front-steps photo does its job with a picture), `meet-security-question` (the reset screen is now
  shown inside `band-name-quiz`'s picture). The ideas of `match-settings`,
  `meet-block-report` and `friendly-stranger` move to the new lesson `privacy-settings`.
  `username-check` (bonus) is dropped; its idea becomes this lesson's "Try this".
- **Needs building:**
  - **Ungraded Play mode** (a simulator with no goal, Continue after a set number of taps) and a
    `post-spread` model whose output is the ring of phones, not meters.
  - **A `social-post` scene** (a photo inside a post, self-labelled, view `front-steps`) for a tap
    hotspot. Fallback: multiple choice with the same picture and text answers.

## 1 · Hook + Play (shared) · `post-and-spread` · not graded · simulator play mode (new)
- **Situation:** Sam's about to post a skateboard photo. Tap Post and watch.
- **Picture:** Sam's phone in the middle, showing a draft post: a photo of a skateboard on a footpath,
  caption "First kickflip!", a big **Post** button. Around it, four smaller friends' phones in a ring,
  blank screens. After Post: the photo flies to all four phones and a counter under Sam's phone reads
  "4 friends". Tapping a friend's phone shows two buttons on it, **Share** and **Screenshot**: Share
  sends the photo on to three tiny phones further out (counter "13 people"); Screenshot flashes white
  and stamps a small camera tag "Saved" on that phone.
- **Action:** Tap Post, then tap a friend's phone. Continue unlocks after Post and one Share or
  Screenshot.

## 2 · Predict · `delete-an-hour-later` · easy · multiple choice with picture
Predict: not scored (any answer, including Not sure, goes straight to the reveal; no hint, no 2nd-wrong step; not counted in first-try accuracy).
- **Situation:** An hour later, Sam deletes the post. What's left?
- **Picture:** The same ring. Sam's phone shows the post with a finger on a red **Delete** button. The
  four friends' phones are dimmed, each with a "?" over it. → **Reveal:** Sam's phone reads "Post
  deleted". Two friends' phones still show the photo, one tagged "Saved" (camera), one tagged "Shared"
  (arrow); the other two are blank.
- **Action:** Pick what's left.
- **Answers:** "Nothing, anywhere" · "Friends' saved copies" · "Only the likes" · "Not sure"
- **What happens:** Friends' saved copies are left.
- **Reveal line:** Here's what happens… deleting removes Sam's copy, but friends saved and shared it in that hour.

## 3 · Name it · `meet-footprint` · explainer with picture
- **Says:** Everything you post adds to your **digital footprint**: the trail you leave online. Copies
  can last, even after you delete.
- **Picture:** The reveal picture from card 2, now with a dotted trail of small footprints from Sam's
  phone to the "Saved" and "Shared" phones, labelled "digital footprint". Sam's phone keeps its
  "Post deleted" tag.

## 4 · Easy win · `front-steps-photo` · easy · hotspot tap on `social-post` (new)
- **Situation:** Sam's next photo, taken on the front steps at home, could last for years.
- **Picture:** The photo fills the card, framed as a post by PixelFox: brick front steps, a front door,
  a letterbox with a big "12", a street sign by the gate reading "Fern St", Sam's skateboard on the
  steps, Sam's sneakers (no face), a pot plant. Every tappable thing has the usual faint ring.
- **Action:** Tap the 2 things that show where Sam lives.
- **Answers:** the letterbox, the street sign, the skateboard, the sneakers, the pot plant
- **Correct:** the letterbox ("12") and the street sign ("Fern St")
- **Right:** A house number and a street name lead a stranger straight to Sam's door.
- **1st wrong:** Imagine a stranger trying to walk to this house: what would help them?
- **2nd wrong:** The letterbox and the street sign: together they're Sam's address.

## 5 · Twist · `band-name-quiz` · medium · multiple choice with picture · ★ module teaser card
- **Situation:** A fun quiz asks for your first pet and your street.
- **Picture:** A phone, two screens stacked. Top: a quiz post from "QuizTime" with a star burst:
  "Pop star name = first pet + street!" and two replies, "Biscuit Wattle" and "Mochi Hill". Bottom, a
  smaller screen: your account, "Forgot password?", "Security question: first pet's name?" →
  **Reveal:** the quiz post scrolled half out of view, and the bottom screen with a closed lock and
  "Still locked".
- **Action:** Pick what you'd do.
- **Answers:** "Reply with the street only" · "Reply with the pet only" · "Scroll past, reply with
  nothing"
- **Correct:** Scroll past, reply with nothing
- **Right:** Your street can find you, and your first pet can unlock a password reset.
- **1st wrong:** (street only) Look at the bottom screen: what does your account ask? (pet only) Could
  a street name help a stranger find someone?
- **2nd wrong:** Scroll past: the street finds you and the first pet unlocks the reset.
- **Teaser note:** works on its own on the "What's next" screen; both screens are in the picture, so it
  needs nothing from earlier cards. (Both hints make sense without card 4.)

## 6 · Real life · `post-while-away` · stretch · multiple choice with picture
- **Situation:** You're on a beach holiday with a great photo to post.
- **Picture:** Your phone with a draft post: a sunny beach, a sandcastle, no people, no place names, a
  **Post** button and a small clock "2:15 pm". → **Reveal:** the same photo posted with a tag "Posted
  from home", and beside it a small house with its lights on.
- **Action:** Pick when to post it.
- **Answers:** "Right now, while it's sunny" · "Tonight, with no caption" · "When you're back home"
- **Correct:** When you're back home
- **Right:** Posting later keeps where you are, and your empty home, to yourself.
- **1st wrong:** The photo shows no address, but a post says something about right now: what?
- **2nd wrong:** Post once you're home: posting now shows where you are and that home is empty.

## 7 · Wrap · `recap`
- **Takeaway:** Your posts can last, so check each one can't find you or unlock your accounts.
- **Try this:** Read your own bio and username, and take out your school, street or full name.
- **Next:** Four switches that decide who can see your posts at all.
