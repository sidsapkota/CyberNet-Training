# Strong Passwords (`strong-passwords`)

- **Module:** 1 · Lock Your Accounts · Free (the course's "Start here" lesson, open to guests)
- **The one idea:** A long password made of random words is the hardest kind for a scammer's computer to guess.
- **The story:** Mia is making a password for her new Game Club account. On screen all lesson: her
  phone with a password box, and beside it the **guessing computer** (a plain grey laptop with a
  dial and a timer, labelled "Scammer's computer"). Each card changes one thing: the password in the box.
- **Cards:** 7 · difficulty: easy, easy, medium, stretch
- **From today's lesson:** keeps `make-it-last` (now the ungraded Play), `meet-passphrase` (Name it),
  `easy-or-hard` (Easy win) and `recap`. New: `password-race` (Predict), `symbol-swap` (Twist),
  `your-new-passphrase` (Real life). Dropped: `meet-guess-lists` (its facts now arrive in the Predict
  reveal and Name it) and `short-but-mixed` (character types muddle the one idea, "long and random").
  **Split:** `the-leak` (one password per account, change email first) is a second idea and moves to
  the new lesson `one-password-each`.
- **Needs building:** a **play mode for the simulator** (no goal: Continue unlocks after the first
  drag, nothing graded) for card 1.

## 1 · Hook + Play (one card)
The Hook and the Play share this card, so a brand-new visitor is doing something on the very first
screen. `make-it-last`, `password` model, length slider 4–20, starting at 6.
- **Situation:** Mia's making a password for her new game account.
- **Picture:** Left: Mia's phone showing "Game Club" and "Create a password", with the password
  box filled by random lowercase letters that grow as the slider moves (`kqzmwp` at 6). Right: the
  guessing computer, a grey laptop with a dial, a label "Scammer's computer" and under it "1 billion
  guesses a second". Its timer reads "Guessed in: less than a second". Under both: the slider,
  "Length: 6 letters", and one small line "+1 letter = 26× more guesses". As Mia's password grows the
  dial slows and the timer climbs: 6 letters "less than a second", 10 "2 days", 14 "about
  2,044 years", 20 "about 632 billion years".
- **Action:** Drag the slider and watch the timer.

## 3 · Predict
`password-race` · multiple choice with a picture and a reveal picture.
- **Situation:** Three passwords race the guessing computer. Which lasts longest?
- **Picture:** The guessing computer in the middle, with three password cards lined up in front of
  it like runners: `Sunshine2026`, `kqzmwp`, `violet cactus harbour pepper`. Each card has an empty
  timer under it. → **Reveal:** `Sunshine2026` is stamped "Guessed: on the guess list!" (a common
  word plus a year), `kqzmwp` is stamped "Guessed: too short", and `violet cactus harbour pepper`
  still shows "Still guessing…" with the dial spinning.
- **Action:** Tap the password that lasts longest.
- **Answers:** the three password cards.
- **Correct:** `violet cactus harbour pepper`
- **Right:** Yes: it's long, and those four words together aren't on the guess list.
- **1st wrong:** Think about what the slider showed you: what made the timer climb?
- **2nd wrong:** It's `violet cactus harbour pepper`: long and random beats short or predictable.

## 4 · Name it
`meet-passphrase`
- **Says:** Scammers' computers try a **guess list** first: common passwords, names, years and song
  lines. A **passphrase** is four or more random words: long, and not on the guess list.
- **Picture:** The same race, now labelled: an arrow to `Sunshine2026` saying "guess list", and a
  bracket over `violet cactus harbour pepper` saying "passphrase: 4 random words".

## 5 · Easy win
`easy-or-hard` · sort bins (4 items).
- **Situation:** Mia jots down four password ideas. Sort each one.
- **Picture:** Four sticky notes stuck around Mia's phone, each with one idea written on it:
  `password123`, `Biscuit2012` (with a tiny dog doodle: her dog's name and birth year), "A line
  from a famous song", `lantern mango velvet quietly`. Two bins under them: "On the guess list" and
  "Hard to guess".
- **Action:** Drag each note into a bin.
- **Answers:** the four sticky notes.
- **Correct:** On the guess list: `password123`, `Biscuit2012`, the song line. Hard to guess:
  `lantern mango velvet quietly`.
- **Right:** Spot on: common passwords, names, years and songs get tried first.
- **1st wrong:** For each note, ask: would a scammer's computer try it early?
- **2nd wrong:** Only the four random words are hard to guess; the others are all on the guess list.

## 6 · Twist
`symbol-swap` · multiple choice with a picture and a reveal picture (2 big buttons).
- **Situation:** Mia swaps letters for symbols and numbers: `$unsh1ne2026`.
- **Picture:** Mia's phone, the password box showing `$unsh1ne2026` with the `$` and `1` lightly
  highlighted; the guessing computer beside it, timer empty. → **Reveal:** the computer's screen
  shows a list scrolling past "sunshine2026 … Sunshine2026 … $unsh1ne2026 ✓" and the stamp
  "Guessed: on the guess list!".
- **Action:** Tap what happens.
- **Answers:** "On the guess list" · "Hard to guess"
- **Correct:** On the guess list
- **Right:** Right: scammers know the swaps people make, so they're on the guess list too.
- **1st wrong:** Under the swaps, what word is it still?
- **2nd wrong:** On the guess list: it's still "sunshine" and a year, and swaps like `$` for S get tried first.

## 7 · Real life
`your-new-passphrase` · multiple choice, the answers drawn as three password cards.
- **Situation:** You need a new password for your email. Pick the strongest.
- **Picture:** Your phone (no Mia now) showing an email app's "New password" screen, with three
  password cards above it: `Luna2011!` (a tiny cat doodle: a pet and a year), `sun cat dog`,
  `otter planet brick soup`.
- **Action:** Tap the password you'd use.
- **Answers:** the three password cards.
- **Correct:** `otter planet brick soup`
- **Right:** Yes: four random words, long and not on the guess list.
- **1st wrong:** Check each one against the passphrase rule: how many random words?
- **2nd wrong:** It's `otter planet brick soup`: `sun cat dog` is only three short words, and a pet
  plus a year is on the guess list.

## 8 · Wrap
`recap`
- **Takeaway:** Long and random wins: a passphrase is four or more random words.
- **Try this:** Make up four random words that don't go together, ready for your next new password.
  Keep them to yourself.
