# Deepfake Scams (`deepfake-scams`)

- **Module:** 4 · When Things Go Wrong · Free (help module, open to guests)
- **The one idea:** a voice or face you know can be faked with AI, so check on the number you already
  have.
- **The story:** your phone and Grandma. A call from an unknown number sounds just like her and asks
  for money. Grandma's real number is saved in your phone as "Grandma". Each card changes one thing:
  where the voice came from, how you check, then her face on a video call, then a face you trust
  asking for a secret.
- **Cards:** 8 · difficulty: Predict easy, Easy win easy, Twist medium, Real life stretch
- **From today's lesson:** one idea now. Kept: `few-seconds-of-voice` (Predict), `best-check` (now the
  Twist and core: Grandma's face on a video call asking for money, instead of a friend asking for a

  `deepfake-recap` (Wrap). `grandma-call` becomes the Hook (not graded). New: `grandma-calling`

## 1 · Hook (`grandma-call`)
- **Situation:** Your phone rings. Unknown number. It sounds exactly like Grandma.
- **Picture:** an incoming-call screen. At the top, "Unknown number" (no digits). A sound-wave icon
  and the bubble "It's Grandma! I've lost my purse."
- **Action:** Tap to answer.

## 2 · Play (`grandma-calling`, not graded: hotspot explore on `phone-call`, view `voice`)
- **Situation:** Listen to the call. Tap each part to look closer.
- **Picture:** the call screen fills the card. Four tappable parts, each with the same faint hollow
  ring:
  - "Unknown number"
  - the sound-wave icon, labelled "Grandma's voice"
  - bubble: "Send me $200 now."
  - bubble: "Don't tell your parents."
- **Action:** Tap each part.
- **What each tap shows:**
  - Unknown number: "Not the number saved as Grandma in your phone."
  - Grandma's voice: "Sounds just like her."
  - "Send me $200 now.": "Hurry and money: a warning sign. Scammers rush you so you don't check."
  - "Don't tell your parents.": "A secret: a warning sign. Scammers want you on your own."
- Continue unlocks once all four have been tapped.

## 3 · Predict (`few-seconds-of-voice`, easy · multiple choice with picture)
Predict: not scored (any answer, including Not sure, goes straight to the reveal; no hint, no 2nd-wrong step; not counted in first-try accuracy).
- **Situation:** A scammer copies a few seconds of Grandma's voice from a video.
- **Picture:** left, a social post: Grandma blowing out birthday candles, a play button and "0:05".
  An arrow to a laptop on the right. → **Reveal:** the laptop's speaker shows a sound wave and the
  bubble "I've lost my purse. Send me $200 now." in Grandma's voice.
- **Action:** Tap what they can make.
- **Answers:** "Her voice, saying anything they type" · "Only that same clip, word for word" ·
  "Nothing: copying a voice takes hours of recordings" · "Not sure"
- **What happens:** "Her voice, saying anything they type".
- **Reveal line:** Here's what happens… AI learns how Grandma sounds from a short clip, then makes her voice say new things.

## 4 · Name it (`meet-deepfake`, explainer)
- **Says:** A voice, face or video faked with AI is a **deepfake**. A voice you know proves nothing,
  so hang up and call the number you already have.
- **Picture:** the call screen from Play, now labelled: "Grandma's voice" tagged "Could be a
  deepfake" (amber), and beside the phone a contact card "Grandma (saved)" tagged "Call this one".

## 5 · Easy win (`call-saved-number`, easy · hotspot tap on `phone-call`, view `after`)
- **Situation:** You've hung up. Now you want to check it was really Grandma.
- **Picture:** the phone after the call, with three big buttons, each with the same hollow ring:
  "Call back Unknown number" · "Call Grandma (saved)" · "Text Unknown number: Is it you?"
  → **Reveal:** Grandma on her own sofa, answering: "Hello love! My purse? It's right here."
- **Action:** Tap how you check.
- **Answers:** the three buttons.
- **Correct:** "Call Grandma (saved)"
- **Right:** Her saved number reaches the real Grandma, and the scammer can't answer it.
- **1st wrong:** Which number belongs to the real Grandma, not the caller?
- **2nd wrong:** Call Grandma's saved number: the unknown number belongs to the caller, who'd just
  answer again.

## 6 · Twist (`best-check`, medium · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** Next day, a video call. Grandma's face asks for $200, right now.
- **Picture:** a video-call screen (`phone-call`, view `video`): Grandma's drawn face filling the
  frame, "Unknown number" at the top, and the bubble "Quick, love, send it now!"
  → **Reveal:** the call ends; Grandma, at home, answers her saved number with a puzzled smile.
- **Action:** Tap what you do.
- **Answers:** "Hang up and call her saved number" · "Send it: you can see her face" · "Ask for
  another video to prove it"
- **Correct:** "Hang up and call her saved number"
- **Right:** Faces can be faked too, but a deepfake can't answer Grandma's real number.
- **1st wrong:** Faces can be copied, just like voices. What can't a scammer fake?
- **2nd wrong:** Hang up and call her saved number: a deepfake can make another video, but it can't
  answer her real phone.

## 7 · Real life (`coach-video`, stretch · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** A video message from an account with your coach's face.
- **Picture:** a chat screen. At the top, an account name "Coach" with a round avatar (a drawn person
  in a cap with a whistle). A video bubble showing the same face, and under it the text: "Send me a
  photo of you in your uniform. Keep it between us."
- **Action:** Tap what you do.
- **Answers:** "Don't reply; tell a trusted adult" · "Send it: it's Coach" · "Ask for another video
  to prove it"
- **Correct:** "Don't reply; tell a trusted adult"
- **Right:** "Keep it between us" is a warning sign, whatever the face, and a trusted adult can check.
- **1st wrong:** Look past the face. Which warning sign is in the message itself?
- **2nd wrong:** Don't reply, and tell a trusted adult: real or fake, a secret photo request is a
  warning sign.

## 8 · Wrap (`deepfake-recap`)
- **Takeaway:** A voice or face you know can be faked: hang up and check on the number you already
  have, or ask a trusted adult.
- **Try this:** Agree a secret family word tonight, to check calls like this.
- **Next:** Something mean online: what to save before you block.
