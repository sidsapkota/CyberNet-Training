# Signs of a Take-Over (`signs-of-a-hack`)

- **Module:** 4 · When Things Go Wrong · Free (help module, open to guests)
- **The one idea:** anything your account did that wasn't you is a warning sign of a take-over.
- **The story:** your phone, your chat app ("Chat") and its "Recent activity" list, and your friend Jai.
  A laptop you don't own signed in at 2:13 am and sent Jai a link. Each card changes one thing: a new
  line in the list, a new day, a code that arrives out of nowhere.
- **Cards:** 8 · difficulty: Predict easy, Easy win easy, Twist medium, Real life stretch
- **From today's lesson:** split in two. This lesson keeps the id `signs-of-a-hack` and only teaches
  spotting a take-over. The recovery steps (`step-by-step`, `order-the-steps`, `weird-messages`,
  `lock-them-out`, `pay-to-unlock`, `recovery-changed`) move to the new lesson
  **`take-back-your-account`**. Kept: `hacked-or-normal` (reworked as the Real life card, bins renamed
  "Warning sign" / "That was me") and `recap`. New: `jai-weird-link`, `recent-activity`,
  `do-nothing-tonight`, `meet-take-over`, `tap-not-you`, `code-out-of-nowhere`. Proposed title change:
  "Signs of a Take-Over" (so the lesson uses one word, "take-over", throughout; the id stays). The
  module description ("Signs of a hack…") would change to match.
- **Needs building:** a new self-labelled scene **`account`**: a generic phone screen of the Chat app's
  account pages, with a view `activity` (a "Recent activity" list whose rows are tappable parts) and a
  view `security` (used by `take-back-your-account`). Explore mode (Play) and tap mode (Easy win) on it
  already exist as hotspot modes. Simplification to record in REVIEW.md: real apps word and lay out
  this list differently.

## 1 · Hook (`jai-weird-link`)
- **Situation:** 7:04 am. Your friend Jai has sent you a message.
- **Picture:** a phone lock screen. Big clock "7:04". One notification card: a round "J" avatar,
  "Jai", and the text "Why did you send me this weird link??". Under it, a grey link chip:
  "prize-win.example/free".
- **Action:** Tap the message to start.

## 2 · Play (`recent-activity`, not graded: hotspot explore on `account`, view `activity`)
- **Situation:** Your Chat app keeps a list of what your account did.
- **Picture:** the phone now shows Chat's "Recent activity" page. Four rows, each with a small device
  icon, newest at the bottom:
  1. "Code sent · Yesterday 4:01 pm"
  2. "Sign-in · This phone · Yesterday 4:02 pm"
  3. "Sign-in · Laptop · Today 2:13 am"
  4. "Sign-in · This phone · Today 7:04 am"
  Each row has a faint hollow ring (all rows, so none stands out).
- **Action:** Tap each line.
- **What each tap shows** (callout pinned inside the panel):
  1. "The app texts a code after you type your password, to check it's you. You were signing in."
  2. "You, after school."
  3. "A laptop you don't own, while you were asleep. Someone else signed in with your password."
  4. "You, this morning, opening Jai's message."
- Continue unlocks once all four have been tapped.

## 3 · Predict (`do-nothing-tonight`, easy · multiple choice with picture)
Predict: not scored (any answer, including Not sure, goes straight to the reveal; no hint, no 2nd-wrong step; not counted in first-try accuracy).
- **Situation:** Whoever got in still has your password. You do nothing.
- **Picture:** the same activity list, the 2:13 am laptop row lightly outlined. A small moon in the
  corner. → **Reveal:** a new row at the bottom, "Sign-in · Laptop · Tonight 1:48 am", and a message
  bubble from Jai over the list: "You sent it again?"
- **Action:** Tap what happens tonight.
- **Answers:** "They sign in again and send more links" · "The app throws them out on its own" ·
  "They can't: a password only works once" · "Not sure"
- **What happens:** "They sign in again and send more links".
- **Reveal line:** Here's what happens… your password still works for them, so they come back tonight.

## 4 · Name it (`meet-take-over`, explainer)
- **Says:** Someone else using your account is a **take-over**. The warning sign is anything your
  account did that **wasn't you**.
- **Picture:** the same activity list, now with a tag on every row: "You" on rows 1, 2 and 4 (plain
  grey tag), "Not you" on the laptop row (amber tag with a warning-triangle icon).

## 5 · Easy win (`tap-not-you`, easy · hotspot tap on `account`, view `activity-2`)
- **Situation:** A new day. You only ever use your phone.
- **Picture:** a fresh activity list, three rows:
  "Code sent · Today 8:09 am" · "Sign-in · This phone · Today 8:10 am" ·
  "Sign-in · Tablet · Today 11:52 am". Every row has the same hollow ring.
- **Action:** Tap the sign-in that wasn't you.
- **Answers:** the three rows (tappable).
- **Correct:** "Sign-in · Tablet · Today 11:52 am"
- **Right:** You don't own a tablet, so that sign-in wasn't you.
- **1st wrong:** Look at the device on each line. Which one isn't yours?
- **2nd wrong:** It's the tablet: you only use your phone, so someone else signed in.

## 6 · Twist (`code-out-of-nowhere`, medium · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** You're watching TV, not signing in. A Chat code pops up.
- **Picture:** a sofa and a glowing TV. Your phone on the cushion shows one notification:
  "Chat code: 482 913". → **Reveal:** the activity list slides up with a new row "Code sent ·
  Today 7:30 pm" tagged "Not you" (amber), and no sign-in from you after it.
- **Action:** Tap what this code means.
- **Answers:** "Warning sign: someone else typed your password" · "Normal: apps send codes now and
  then" · "Normal: it's reminding you to sign in"
- **Correct:** "Warning sign: someone else typed your password"
- **Right:** A code comes after someone types your password, and this time it wasn't you.
- **1st wrong:** This morning, the 8:09 code came just before your own sign-in. Who's signing in now?
- **2nd wrong:** It's a warning sign: someone else typed your password. Never share that code.

## 7 · Real life (`hacked-or-normal`, stretch · sort bins)
- **Situation:** Your own accounts this week. Some alerts are you, some aren't.
- **Picture:** a stack of four notification cards above two bins, "Warning sign" (amber outline) and
  "That was me" (grey outline). Cards:
  - "Sign-in alert, just after you set up a new phone"
  - "Friends got a link you never sent"
  - "A post on your page you didn't make"
  - "App asks you to sign in again after an update"
- **Action:** Sort each alert into a bin.
- **Correct:** Warning sign: the link and the post. That was me: the new-phone alert and the sign-in
  again.
- **Right:** Anything that wasn't you is a warning sign; an alert that matches what you just did is you.
- **1st wrong:** For each one, ask: did I just do this, or did someone else?
- **2nd wrong:** The link and the post weren't you, so they're warning signs. The other two match
  something you did.

## 8 · Wrap (`recap`)
- **Takeaway:** Anything your account did that wasn't you is a warning sign. Spot one? Tell a trusted
  adult.
- **Try this:** Open one app's settings and find the list of devices signed in to your account.
- **Next:** How to kick whoever got in out of your account, and keep them out.
