# Stay Safe Online: Course Final (`stay-safe-online-final`)

- **Module:** 4 · When Things Go Wrong · Free (open to guests) · the course final
- **Questions:** 8, one try each, no hints; pass at 70% (6 of 8). The results screen shows each
  explanation.
- **What it tests:** module 1 (free; matched to its new storyboards: `strong-passwords`, `two-step-sign-in`, `strongest-lock`) and every lesson of module 4 (free), in course order. Modules 2
  and 3 are left out on purpose, as today (REVIEW.md: "The course final (open to guests) tests only
  Modules 1 and 4"): this module is open to guests, who can't open the Pro modules, so the final only
  asks what a free learner has been able to play. If the owner would rather test modules 2 and 3 too,
  swap Q3 or Q4 for one of them.
- **From today's quiz:** kept: `q-strong-or-easy`, `q-two-step`, `q-code`, `q-hacked` (bins renamed to
  match the lesson), `q-music-takeover` (second step is now the device still signed in, so it doesn't
  copy the lesson's recovery-email card), `q-voice-message` (now asks for money, not gift card codes,
  so "code" keeps one meaning) and `q-where`. New: `q-block-first` (`save-the-evidence`). Dropped:
  `q-trusted` (a trusted adult is now part of the right answer in Q5 and Q7). None copies a lesson
  card word for word.
- **Needs building:** nothing new (quiz pictures are plain illustrations; Q4 and Q8 reuse the drawing
  style of the `account` and `help-board` scenes).

## Q1 · `q-strong-or-easy` (Strong Passwords · sort bins)
- **Situation:** Four passwords on sticky notes. Some are much easier to guess.
- **Picture:** four sticky notes on a desk above two bins, "Strong" and "Easy to guess". Notes:
  "frost banjo tulip ladder" · "Somewhere over the rainbow" · "BuddyTheDogBorn2015" · "Sunsh1ne!"
- **Action:** Sort each password into a bin.
- **Correct:** Strong: "frost banjo tulip ladder". Easy to guess: the other three.
- **Explanation:** Four random words are long and hard to predict; song lines, pet names with years and
  short words with swapped symbols are guessed early.

## Q2 · `q-two-step` (Two-Step Sign-In and The Strongest Lock · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** Your password leaked, but two-step sign-in is on.
- **Picture:** a stranger's laptop on a sign-in page: the password box is filled with dots, and a
  second box reads "Code from your authenticator app" and is empty. Your phone sits in your own pocket,
  showing its authenticator app.
- **Action:** Tap what else they'd need to get in.
- **Answers:** "The code from the authenticator app on your phone" · "Nothing more: the password gets them in" ·
  "Just your username, which is easy to find"
- **Correct:** "The code from the authenticator app on your phone"
- **Explanation:** With two-step sign-in, a stolen password isn't enough: they'd also need the code
  from your phone.

## Q3 · `q-code` (Two-Step Sign-In · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** Your cousin's account: "I entered you in a giveaway! Send me your code."
- **Picture:** a chat with "Cousin Ana" (round avatar) showing that message. On top, a notification
  slides down: "Sign-in code: 615 204".
- **Action:** Tap what you do.
- **Answers:** "Don't send it; warn your cousin another way" · "Send it: it's your cousin" · "Send it,
  then change your password"
- **Correct:** "Don't send it; warn your cousin another way"
- **Explanation:** A sign-in code opens your account, so never send it, even to family: their account
  may have been taken over.

## Q4 · `q-hacked` (Signs of a Take-Over · sort bins)
- **Situation:** Alerts from your shopping account this week.
- **Picture:** four notification cards above two bins, "Warning sign" and "That was me": "An order you
  didn't place" · "A laptop you don't own signed in" · "A receipt for what you just bought" · "A code
  as you sign in"
- **Action:** Sort each alert into a bin.
- **Correct:** Warning sign: the order and the laptop. That was me: the receipt and the code.
- **Explanation:** Anything that wasn't you is a warning sign; a receipt or code that matches what you
  just did is you.

## Q5 · `q-music-takeover` (Take Your Account Back · scenario, two steps)
- **Situation:** Your music app plays songs you never picked. You can still sign in.
- **Picture:** a phone on a music app's "Recently played" list, with three songs marked "Not you".
- **Step 1 · Action:** Tap what you do first.
  - **Answers:** "Tell a trusted adult, and change the password" · "Wait a week and see" · "Start a
    new account and forget the old one"
  - **Correct:** "Tell a trusted adult, and change the password"
- **Step 2 · Situation:** Password changed. A tablet you don't own is still signed in.
  - **Picture:** the app's "Where you're signed in" list: "This phone" and "Tablet", each with
    "Sign out".
  - **Action:** Tap what you do next.
  - **Answers:** "Sign out the tablet" · "Leave it: the password is new now"
  - **Correct:** "Sign out the tablet"
- **Explanation:** Tell a trusted adult and change the password, then sign out other devices: a device
  that's already in may stay in.

## Q6 · `q-voice-message` (Deepfake Scams · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** A new number. A voice message in your aunt's voice: "Send $100, quick!"
- **Picture:** a phone showing a voice message (sound wave, "0:06") from "New number". Beside the phone,
  a saved contact card: "Aunt Rosa".
- **Action:** Tap what you do.
- **Answers:** "Call Aunt Rosa on her saved number" · "Send it: it's her voice" · "Reply to the new
  number to ask if it's her"
- **Correct:** "Call Aunt Rosa on her saved number"
- **Explanation:** A voice can be a deepfake and the new number belongs to the sender, so check on the
  number you already have.

## Q7 · `q-block-first` (Save the Evidence · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** A player in your game keeps sending you nasty messages.
- **Picture:** a game's chat window: three messages from "rex_404", the time "Sunday 6:15 pm", and a
  "Block" button at the top.
- **Action:** Tap what you do first.
- **Answers:** "Screenshot the chat, then block and tell a trusted adult" · "Block straight away, then
  screenshot the chat" · "Delete the chat, then block them"
- **Correct:** "Screenshot the chat, then block and tell a trusted adult"
- **Explanation:** Blocking can hide the chat, so screenshot first; then block, report and tell a
  trusted adult.

## Q8 · `q-where` (Getting Help · match pairs)
- **Situation:** Four problems. Each has its own helper.
- **Picture:** four problem cards on the left and four helper tiles on the right, drawn like the "Who
  can help?" board.
- **Action:** Match each problem to its helper.
- **Answers (pairs):** "Your card used online" → "Your bank, now" · "A scam text, to warn others" →
  "Scamwatch" · "Someone may get hurt now" → "Call 000" · "Your ID details stolen" → "IDCARE"
- **Correct:** as listed.
- **Explanation:** Your bank straight away for your card, Scamwatch for scams, 000 for danger right now,
  and IDCARE for stolen details.
