## 1 · Hook + Play (shared) · `permission-panel` · not graded · simulator play mode (new)
- **Situation:** Sam installs a drawing game. Flip each switch to see what it can reach.
- **Picture:** Sam's phone showing a panel "Drawing game can use:" with four switches, all off, each
  with an icon: Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts. Switching one on fills a small window beside it:
  Camera → a live view of Sam's desk, "Can take photos"; Microphone → a moving sound wave, "Can hear";
  Location → a map pin on a street map, "Can see where you are"; Contacts → a list "Jo, Ali, Mum…"
  with "214 contacts".
- **Action:** Flip the switches. Continue unlocks after two flips.

## 2 · Predict · `new-game-asks` · easy · multiple choice with picture
Predict: not scored (any answer, including Not sure, goes straight to the reveal; no hint, no 2nd-wrong step; not counted in first-try accuracy).
- **Situation:** The drawing game asks for Sam's contacts. Sam taps Don't allow.

- **Answers:** "It still draws just fine" · "It stops working" · "It deletes the drawing" · "Not sure"

- **Reveal line:** Here's what happens… drawing never needed your contacts, so the game works fine without them.

## 3 · Name it · `meet-permission` · explainer with picture
- **Says:** An **app permission** is your OK for an app to use your camera, microphone, location or
  contacts. Only allow what it needs, and change it any time in Settings.
- **Picture:** The same pop-up from card 2, labelled "app permission", with the four icons from card 1
  in a row under it and a small gear "Settings" with an arrow, "change any time".

## 4 · Easy win · `needs-it` · easy · sort bins
- **Situation:** Four apps each ask for one thing. Which ones need it?
- **Picture:** Sort bins "Needs it" and "Doesn't need it". Each item is an app icon, an arrow and a
  permission icon, with short words under it.
- **Action:** Drag each app into Needs it or Doesn't need it.
- **Answers:** "Maps → location" · "Video call → microphone" · "Torch → contacts" · "Calculator →
  location"
- **Correct:** Needs it: Maps → location, Video call → microphone. Doesn't need it: Torch → contacts,
  Calculator → location.
- **Right:** Allow what fits the app's job: a torch needs light, not your contacts.
- **1st wrong:** For each one, ask: could the app do its main job without it?
- **2nd wrong:** Maps and video calls need theirs; a torch and a calculator don't need contacts or
  location.

## 5 · Twist · `directions-to-the-park` · medium · tap a part of the picture (new; fallback multiple choice)
- **Situation:** Sam wants map directions to the skate park. The map asks for location.
- **Picture:** A map app on Sam's phone with a pin "Skate park". Over it, a pop-up "Allow Maps to use
  your location?" with three buttons: **Always (even when closed)**, **Only while using the app**,
  **Don't allow**. → **Reveal:** a blue route line to the skate park, and beside it a small closed phone
  with a crossed-out pin, "Closed: can't see you".
- **Action:** Tap the best button.
- **Answers:** the three buttons
- **Correct:** Only while using the app
- **Right:** The directions work, and once the map's closed it can't see where Sam is.
- **1st wrong:** (Always) Does the map need to know where Sam is when it's closed? (Don't allow) Can a
  map give directions without knowing where Sam is?
- **2nd wrong:** Only while using the app: the map works, and it can't follow Sam once it's closed.

## 6 · Real life · `mic-dot` · stretch · multiple choice with picture
- **Situation:** This dot means an app is using your mic. You're not on a call.
- **Picture:** The top of your phone screen, zoomed in: the time "3:45", battery, and a small glowing
  dot with a label arrow "mic in use (on many phones)". Below, your home screen with no call open. → **Reveal:**
  Settings open at "Microphone", a list of apps with switches, one app ("Sticker app") switched off, and
  the dot gone.
- **Action:** Pick what you do.
- **Answers:** "Ignore it: dots are normal" · "Restart the phone" · "Find the app, switch its mic off in
  Settings"
- **Correct:** Find the app, switch its mic off in Settings
- **Right:** Settings takes the permission back, so that app can't use the mic again.
- **1st wrong:** (ignore) No call is on: should any app be using your mic right now? (restart) After a
  restart, would the app still have its permission?
- **2nd wrong:** Find the app and switch its mic off in Settings: restarting doesn't take a permission
  back.
