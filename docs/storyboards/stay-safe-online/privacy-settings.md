# Privacy Settings (`privacy-settings`) · NEW lesson, split from `your-digital-footprint`

- **Module:** 3 · Guard Your Privacy · Pro
- **The one idea:** Privacy settings choose who can see your posts and who can reach you; for anyone
  you've already let in, block, report and tell a trusted adult.
- **The story:** Sam's profile (PixelFox) from the last lesson, shown two ways all lesson: Sam's
  **Settings** screen with its switches on the left, and a **Stranger's view** phone on the right that
  shows what someone who doesn't follow Sam sees. Each card flips one switch or adds one person.
- **Cards:** 7 (Hook and Play share card 1) · difficulty: easy, easy, medium, stretch
- **From today's lesson:** a new lesson (proposed id `privacy-settings`, order 2 in the module, before
  `apps-and-wi-fi`; `about` "Choose who sees your posts and who can reach you."; icon e.g. a shield)
  built from the second half of today's `your-digital-footprint`. Being a new lesson,
  it has no saved progress, so every card id is fresh: `flip-the-switches`, `stranger-messages`,
  `meet-privacy-settings`, `tag-review` (the idea of `match-settings`), `friendly-stranger` (same idea
  and name, rebuilt as multiple choice with a picture), `screenshot-private-post`, `recap`. Dropped:
  `meet-block-report` (Block and Report are shown inside `friendly-stranger`'s picture).
- **Needs building:**
  - **Ungraded Play mode** (as in `your-digital-footprint`) and a `privacy-switches` model: toggle
    controls, output = the Stranger's view phone.
  - **Answers that are tappable parts of a picture** (the switches on the Settings screen) for card 4.
    Fallback: multiple choice with the picture and the switch names as text.

## 1 · Hook + Play (shared) · `flip-the-switches` · not graded · simulator play mode (new)
- **Situation:** Sam's account is public. Flip a switch and watch what strangers see.
- **Picture:** Left, Sam's Settings: three switches, all set the unsafe way: "Private account" (off),
  "Share location" (on), "Review tags" (off). Right, the Stranger's view of PixelFox: a grid of three
  skate photos, a pin chip "Near Fern Park" under the newest one, and a "Tagged" row with a photo
  "Tagged by Jo". Flipping a switch changes the right phone at once: Private account on → the grid
  becomes a closed lock and "Follow to see posts"; Share location off → the pin chip disappears;
  Review tags on → the tagged photo becomes a grey tile "Waiting for PixelFox's OK".
- **Action:** Flip the switches. Continue unlocks after two flips.

## 2 · Predict · `stranger-messages` · easy · multiple choice with picture
- **Situation:** Sam switches on "Messages: friends only". Then a stranger taps Message.
- **Picture:** Left, a fourth switch now added at the bottom, "Messages: friends only", shown turning
  on. Right, the Stranger's view with a message box "hey, cool tricks" and a finger on **Send**; the
  space below is covered by a "?". → **Reveal:** the Send button greyed out and a note "PixelFox only
  gets messages from friends".
- **Action:** Pick what happens.
- **Answers:** "The message reaches Sam" · "The stranger can't send it" · "It sends, with a warning"
- **Correct:** The stranger can't send it
- **Right:** With friends-only messages, people Sam hasn't added can't message at all.
- **1st wrong:** Read the switch's name again: who is allowed to message now?
- **2nd wrong:** The stranger can't send it: only Sam's friends can message now.

## 3 · Name it · `meet-privacy-settings` · explainer with picture
- **Says:** **Privacy settings** choose who can see your posts and who can reach you. Each switch
  controls one thing.
- **Picture:** Sam's Settings with all four switches now on the safe side, each labelled with the one
  thing it controls: "Private account" → "posts", "Messages: friends only" → "messages", "Share
  location" (off) → "location", "Review tags" → "tags". The Stranger's view beside it shows only the
  lock.

## 4 · Easy win · `tag-review` · easy · tap a part of the picture (new; fallback multiple choice)
- **Situation:** Jo tagged Sam in a photo outside school. Sam wants to OK tags first.
- **Picture:** Left, Sam's Settings with the four switches, all off this time. Right, the Stranger's
  view showing the new tagged photo "Tagged by Jo": a school gate, two kids from behind.
- **Action:** Tap the switch that lets Sam OK tags first.
- **Answers:** the four switches: "Private account", "Messages: friends only", "Share location",
  "Review tags"
- **Correct:** Review tags
- **Right:** Review tags keeps photos Sam's tagged in off Sam's profile until Sam says OK.
- **1st wrong:** Which switch is about photos other people tag you in?
- **2nd wrong:** Review tags: tagged photos wait for Sam's OK before they show on Sam's profile.

## 5 · Twist · `friendly-stranger` · medium · multiple choice with picture
- **Situation:** Sam's new game friend asks for Sam's school and a photo.
- **Picture:** A chat on Sam's phone with "GameMate_8" (a game-controller avatar, no face), who is on
  Sam's friends list (a small "Friend" tag). Their message: "what school do u go to? send a pic!" The
  chat's "⋯" menu is open in the corner, showing **Block** and **Report**. → **Reveal:** the chat closed
  with "Blocked" and "Reported", and a small speech bubble from Sam to a grown-up at home: "Can I show
  you something?"
- **Action:** Pick what Sam should do.
- **Answers:** "Make the account private" · "Send a photo, not the school" · "Block, report, tell a
  trusted adult"
- **Correct:** Block, report, tell a trusted adult
- **Right:** Settings can't keep out someone Sam already let in, so block, report and tell a trusted
  adult.
- **1st wrong:** (private) GameMate_8 is already Sam's friend: would a private account keep them out?
  (photo) Think back to the front-steps photo: what can a photo give away?
- **2nd wrong:** Block, report, tell a trusted adult: Sam's not in trouble, and telling someone keeps
  Sam safe.

## 6 · Real life · `screenshot-private-post` · stretch · multiple choice with picture
- **Situation:** Your account is private. A follower screenshots your post to share it.
- **Picture:** Your Settings with "Private account" on. Beside it, a follower's phone with your post and
  a white screenshot flash, and a "Share" arrow lifting off. → **Reveal:** the screenshot showing up on
  three more phones, the same trail of footprints as in the last lesson.
- **Action:** Pick what can stop them sharing it.
- **Answers:** "Your private account" · "Turning on Review tags" · "No setting: followers can copy it"
- **Correct:** No setting: followers can copy it
- **Right:** Settings choose who sees a post, but a follower can still copy it, so post like anyone
  might see.
- **1st wrong:** Think back to the friends' phones in the last lesson: what could they do with a post?
- **2nd wrong:** No setting stops it: a follower can still copy your post, so share only what you'd be
  OK with anyone seeing.

- **Takeaway:** Privacy settings choose who sees and reaches you; if someone crosses the line, block,
  report and tell a trusted adult.
