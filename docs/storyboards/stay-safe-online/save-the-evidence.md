# Save the Evidence (`save-the-evidence`, new lesson)

- **Module:** 4 · When Things Go Wrong · Free (help module, open to guests)
- **The one idea:** before you block, save the evidence: screenshot who it was, what they said and
  when. Then block, report and tell a trusted adult.
- **The story:** your phone and one chat app. In the group chat "Weekend Crew", the account
  "shadow_77" keeps posting mean things about you. Each card changes one thing: you block too soon,
  shadow_77 posts again, then a different account threatens you with a fake picture.
- **Cards:** 8 · difficulty: Predict easy, Easy win easy, Twist medium, Real life stretch
- **From today's lesson:** new lesson, split from `getting-help` (proposed order 4 in the module, after
  `deepfake-scams`; icon `message-square-warning`). Carries over, as new cards in this lesson:
  `block-before-saving` (Predict), `never-your-fault` (now the Name it and the Wrap), `group-chat`
  (Easy win, now one step) and both steps of `fake-image-report` from `deepfake-scams` (the Twist and
  Real life). Every service and its wording stays as verified in REVIEW.md.
- **Needs building:** a new self-labelled scene **`group-chat`**: a generic chat screen with tappable
  parts for the username, a message, the date and time, and the "Block" and "Report" buttons. Views:
  `chat` (messages showing) and `blocked` (empty, "You blocked shadow_77"). Explore mode on it exists.
  The fake-picture cards never show a picture: only a grey box reading "Picture hidden".

## 1 · Hook (`mean-group-chat`)
- **Situation:** In your group chat, someone keeps posting mean things about you.
- **Picture:** a phone showing the group chat "Weekend Crew". Three messages: "shadow_77: just leave
  the chat", "kai_m: lol", "shadow_77: nobody wants you here". A small calm line under the phone:
  "It's never your fault."
- **Action:** Tap to start.

## 2 · Play (`chat-parts`, not graded: hotspot explore on `group-chat`, view `chat`)
- **Situation:** This is the chat. Tap each part to see what it is.
- **Picture:** the same chat filling the card, with five tappable parts, each with the same faint
  hollow ring: the username "shadow_77", the message "nobody wants you here", the time stamp
  "Saturday 8:40 pm", and two buttons at the top, "Block" and "Report".
- **Action:** Tap each part.
- **What each tap shows:**
  - shadow_77: "Who sent it."
  - The message: "What they said."
  - Saturday 8:40 pm: "When it happened."
  - Block: "Stops them messaging you."
  - Report: "Tells the app, so it can remove it."
- Continue unlocks once all five have been tapped.

## 3 · Predict (`block-before-saving`, easy · multiple choice with picture)
Predict: not scored (any answer, including Not sure, goes straight to the reveal; no hint, no 2nd-wrong step; not counted in first-try accuracy).
- **Situation:** You tap Block before saving anything.
- **Picture:** the chat, with a finger pressing "Block". → **Reveal:** the `blocked` view: an empty
  chat with one grey line, "You blocked shadow_77", and no messages left.
- **Action:** Tap what might happen to the messages.
- **Answers:** "They may vanish, leaving nothing to show" · "The app keeps a copy for you" · "Blocking
  reports them too" · "Not sure"
- **What happens:** "They may vanish, leaving nothing to show".
- **Reveal line:** Here's what happens… on this app, blocking hid the whole chat, and the evidence went with it.

## 4 · Name it (`screenshot-first`, explainer)
- **Says:** Before you block, **save the evidence**: screenshot the messages, the username and the
  date. Then block, report it in the app, and tell a trusted adult.
- **Picture:** the chat from Play, now labelled. A dashed frame around the messages, the username and
  the time stamp, marked "1 · Screenshot". The Block button marked "2", the Report button marked "3".

## 5 · Easy win (`group-chat`, easy · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** shadow_77 posts again: "everyone's laughing at you".
- **Picture:** the same chat with the new message at the bottom.
- **Action:** Tap what you do first.
- **Answers:** "Screenshot it, then tell a trusted adult" · "Reply with something just as mean" ·
  "Delete the chat so you don't see it"
- **Correct:** "Screenshot it, then tell a trusted adult"
- **Right:** Now you have the evidence, and someone on your side.
- **1st wrong:** Which choice keeps proof of what they said?
- **2nd wrong:** Screenshot it, then tell a trusted adult: replying can make it worse, and deleting
  loses the evidence.

## 6 · Twist (`fake-picture`, medium · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** A new account says it made a fake picture of you, and wants money.
- **Picture:** a new chat from "unknown_acc" (grey avatar). A grey box with an eye-off icon reading
  "Picture hidden", then the message "Pay $50 or I share it." The calm line "It's never your fault."
  sits under the phone.
- **Action:** Tap what you save.
- **Answers:** "Screenshot the messages and their profile, not the picture" · "Save the picture too,
  as proof" · "Nothing: pay, so it never gets shared"
- **Correct:** "Screenshot the messages and their profile, not the picture"
- **Right:** Save who they are and what they said; never save or share the picture itself.
- **1st wrong:** What shows who did this, without keeping the picture?
- **2nd wrong:** Screenshot the messages and their profile, never the picture. And never pay: paying
  usually leads to more demands.

## 7 · Real life (`fake-image-report`, stretch · multiple choice with picture)
- **Situation:** You're under 18. Where do you report the fake picture?
- **Picture:** your phone beside a trusted adult's hand, both over three plain tiles (no logos):
  "Report button in the app", "eSafety · helps remove it · esafety.gov.au/report" and "ACCCE · helps
  under-18s being threatened · accce.gov.au/report". (The tiles teach what each service does, so the
  card sets itself up; `getting-help` meets them again.)
- **Action:** Tap where you report it.
- **Answers:** "The app, eSafety and the ACCCE" · "Just the app: it's only a fake" · "Nowhere: you
  might get in trouble"
- **Correct:** "The app, eSafety and the ACCCE"
- **Right:** eSafety can help remove it, and the ACCCE helps under-18s who are being threatened.
- **1st wrong:** A fake is still serious, and you won't be in trouble. Who helps under-18s being
  threatened?
- **2nd wrong:** Report it to the app, eSafety and the ACCCE. You will not be in trouble for asking for
  help.

## 8 · Wrap (`recap`)
- **Takeaway:** Screenshot first, then block, report and tell a trusted adult. It's never your fault.
- **Try this:** Practise taking a screenshot on your phone, so you can do it fast.
- **Talk any time:** **Kids Helpline** 1800 55 1800 (ages 5 to 25) or **Lifeline** 13 11 14.
- **Next:** Who to tell, and where to go, whatever goes wrong online.
